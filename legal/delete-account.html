Delete your GoodNeighbour account

App: GoodNeighbour (org.goodneighbour.app) · Developer: Aarohan Rural Development Society · Last updated: 31 October 2026

Delete it inside the app

  1. Open GoodNeighbour and tap your profile picture (top right).
  2. Tap Account.
  3. Read the warning, type DELETE in the box, then tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm in the dialog that appears.

If you signed in a long time ago, the app may ask you to log out, sign in again and then delete. This is a safety step.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in (your Firebase account).
  • Your profile, badges and points, and your notifications.
  • Every post you shared, with its photos, documents and private contact.
  • The help tickets on your posts, with their messages.
  • Offers of help you made on other people's posts, with the messages you wrote there.
  • Your place in the communities you joined, and your giving history shown in the app.
  • Your monthly-giving plans are cancelled and their reminders turned off.

What is kept

Records of payments you reported, and the totals they feed, are kept so that an NGO's and an organiser's accounts stay correct and for any dispute. They are not shown to other users. If you want your name removed from them, email us (below) and we will do it unless the law requires us to keep it. Reports you filed about posts are also kept for safety.

Delete without the app

Email aarohandevelopmentsociety@gmail.com with the subject Delete my GoodNeighbour account, from the same email address you used to register. Tell us if you also want your name removed from the records under "What is kept". We will delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email.

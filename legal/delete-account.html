Delete your GoodNeighbour account

App: GoodNeighbour (org.goodneighbour.app) · Developer: Rutuja Thakre · Last updated: 31 October 2026

Delete it inside the app

  1. Open GoodNeighbour and tap your profile picture (top right).
  2. Tap Account.
  3. Read the warning, type DELETE in the box, then tap Delete my account.
  4. Confirm in the dialog that appears.

If you signed in a long time ago, the app may ask you to log out, sign in again and then delete. This is a safety step.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in (your Firebase account).
  • Your profile, badges and points, and your notifications.
  • Every post you shared, with its photos, documents and private contact.
  • The help tickets on your posts, with their messages.
  • Your own list of help offers and your giving history shown in the app.

What may be kept

The app does not yet remove these automatically. We delete them when you ask us by email (below), except records we need for accounting or the law:

  • Offers of help and messages you wrote on other people's posts.
  • Records of payments you reported and monthly-giving plans, which keep the NGO's and organiser's totals correct.
  • Your entry in a community's list of members, and any reports you filed.

Delete without the app

Email rutujathakre213@gmail.com with the subject Delete my GoodNeighbour account, from the same email address you used to register. Tell us if you also want the items under "What may be kept" removed. We will delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email.

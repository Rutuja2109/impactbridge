GoodNeighbour Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

GoodNeighbour ("the app") is an Android app made by Rutuja Thakre ("we", "us"). It helps people share needs in their neighbourhood and lets neighbours offer time, items or money. This page explains what information the app collects, why, who can see it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

WhatDetails
AccountYour name and email address, from Google Sign-In or from the email and password you register with. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us.
Profile and activityWhen you joined, when you last opened the app, the communities you joined, your badges and points (worked out from your activity), and a device token used to send you notifications.
Posts you shareTitle, description, cause, city and area, the place you pin on the map (latitude, longitude and its address), photos, and for fundraisers a UPI ID to receive money. Your private contact (an email or phone number) and any photos of documents are kept private; see section 3.
Help offers (tickets)Your name and email, an optional phone number, what you offer, when you are free, and the messages you and the organiser write to each other.
GivingAmounts you say you gave, an optional UPI reference number you type, and your monthly-giving plan and reminder day. We never collect card numbers, bank details or your UPI PIN. Payments are made in your own UPI app directly to the NGO or organiser; the app does not hold your money.
LocationOnly when you open the map picker. The app can use your phone's position, after you allow it, to check that you are near the place you pin. We save the pin you confirm and how far your phone was from it. We do not track your movements.
PhotosOnly the pictures you choose with the phone's photo picker. They are made smaller and saved with your post.
ReportsIf you report a post, we keep the report and the reason.

2. How we use it

  • To run the app: sign you in, show posts, let people help and give.
  • To check posts before they appear, and to keep people safe (reviewers see the details in section 3).
  • To connect helpers with organisers and to send you notifications about your posts, help offers and gifts.
  • To count how the app is used (for example how many people joined or posted). These are totals for the administrators.
  • To follow the law and to prevent misuse.

We do not show advertising and we do not sell your information.

3. Who can see what

  • Approved posts (title, description, area, map pin, photos and the name shown as organiser) can be seen by other people who are signed in to the app.
  • Your private contact and photos of documents can be seen only by you and the app's administrators (reviewers).
  • Help offers (your name, email, phone if you gave one, and messages) can be seen by the organiser of that post and by the administrators.
  • Confirmed supporters of a fundraiser are shown as a first name and an amount.
  • Administrators can see posts, help offers, reported payments, reports and usage totals so they can run and moderate the service.

4. Services we use

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore database, Cloud Messaging) stores your account and data on Google's servers. The database is located in Mumbai, India (asia-south1).
  • Google Sign-In, if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • OpenStreetMap provides the map and place search. When you use the map or search for a place, the text you search and the map position are sent to OpenStreetMap's servers. See their privacy policy.
  • Your UPI app (for example PhonePe or Google Pay) handles the actual payment. We do not see it.

5. Security and where data is kept

Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by access rules so people only see what section 3 allows. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not post anything you would not want an approved post to show.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time (see the account deletion page). Some records, such as payments you reported and monthly-giving records, may be kept for accounting and dispute purposes.

7. Your choices

  • Edit or resend your posts: Impact, then My posts, then Edit.
  • Withdraw a help offer from its ticket.
  • Turn off notifications or location in your phone's settings.
  • Delete your account in the app, or ask us by email.
  • Ask us to show, correct or erase your information. This includes your rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

8. Children

The app is not meant for anyone under 18. If you think a child has given us information, email us and we will remove it.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. If a change is important, we will also tell you in the app.
